Introduction

Table tennis is a fast-paced racket sport played on a hard table divided by a net.

Essential Equipment

Basic gear needed for a match:

  • Paddles with rubber sheets
  • Celluloid or plastic polymer balls
  • Standard table with net assembly

Match Rules

Core scoring flow:

  1. Serve switches every two points
  2. Ball must bounce once on each side
  3. First player to eleven points wins
