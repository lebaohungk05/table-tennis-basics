Table Tennis Basics

Table tennis, also known as ping-pong, is a high-speed racket sport played on a hard table divided by a net. It demands rapid reflexes, precise spin control, and tactical agility.

Why Play Table Tennis?

Table tennis offers numerous physical and mental benefits for players of all ages:

  • Improves hand-eye coordination and mental alertness
  • Develops quick reflexes and tactical thinking
  • Provides a great cardiovascular workout with low joint impact
