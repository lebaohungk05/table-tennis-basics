Why I enjoy table tennis

Table tennis is my favorite way to take a break from studying. I enjoy the sound of the ball bouncing across the table and the challenge of keeping a rally going. A friendly game gives me time to move, concentrate, and have fun with friends.

These are the three things I like most about playing:

  • Practicing my timing and trying to return one more shot.
  • Spending time with friends away from a screen.
  • Learning something new each time I pick up a paddle.
