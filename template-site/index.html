Table Tennis Central

A community club for every level, from your first rally to your first tournament.

Get Started

Six Tables, One Community

Table Tennis Central runs six competition tables, open every evening and all weekend. Coaches are on the floor at every session, paddles and balls are free to borrow, and newcomers are paired with a club member for their first visit. Read the rules of play before you come and you will be rallying in minutes.

Our Programs

League Nights

Every Thursday from 7 pm. Players are grouped by rating into divisions of six, play best-of-five matches, and move up or down at the end of each eight-week season.

Beginner Clinics

Four Saturday mornings covering grip, footwork, the forehand drive and a legal serve. Groups of eight, two coaches, all equipment provided.

Junior Academy

For ages 8 to 16, twice a week after school. Multiball drills, doubles play and a friendly ladder competition that keeps every player motivated.
